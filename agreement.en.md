## 1. The service

The app is a client tool that uses the API of a Qinglong panel (an open-source scheduled task platform) you deploy yourself to view and manage that panel's scheduled tasks, environment variables, subscriptions, scripts, dependencies, logs, config files and system information, and to change account and security settings such as the panel password, two-factor authentication and avatar.

The app does not include the Qinglong server and provides no servers, scripts or subscription content. Every feature depends on the Qinglong panel you deploy and configure.

## 2. Acceptable use

- Connect only to Qinglong panels you own or are authorized to manage, and keep panel addresses, passwords and app secrets safe.
- Comply with applicable laws and regulations, including those of the People's Republic of China. Do not use the app or the services it manages to run scripts or tasks that infringe the rights of others, damage computer systems or are otherwise unlawful.
- Actions you perform on the panel through the app (such as running or deleting tasks, editing scripts and configs, installing or removing dependencies, deleting logs, restarting the panel, changing the account password or turning two-factor authentication on or off) are your own decisions and responsibility. Deletions usually cannot be undone, so act with care.
- After turning on two-factor authentication, keep the authenticator secret safe; if you lose it you may be unable to sign in and will need to fix it on the server yourself.
- If you tick "Remember username and password", your credentials are stored encrypted on this device, and anyone who holds and can unlock the device can manage your panel. On shared devices, leave it unticked or sign out when you are done.
- The app keeps the panel data it last read (which may include sensitive content such as environment variables and scripts) on the device for offline viewing. Keep your device secure; sign out, delete the server or uninstall the app to remove this data.
